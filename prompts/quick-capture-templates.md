# Quick Capture Templates

Five-sentence-or-fewer starters designed to trigger clean metadata
classification (`decision`, `person_note`, `insight`, `meeting_debrief`,
`task`). After a week of capturing you probably won't need these — you'll
have your own phrasing patterns — but they help build the habit early.

Capture any of these via:

```
./cli/brain-capture "your filled-in sentence"
```

**Decision**
> Decision: we're [what changed], replacing [what it replaces]. Reason:
> [why]. Effective [when].

**Person note**
> Talked with [name] about [topic]. They mentioned [key fact]. Context:
> [why it matters / what prompted it].

**Insight**
> Insight: [observation], not [what we previously assumed]. Evidence:
> [what showed you this]. Implication: [so what].

**Meeting debrief**
> Meeting with [people] about [topic]. Outcome: [decision/status]. Action
> items: [who does what by when].

**Task**
> Need to [action] by [when], because [why]. Blocked on: [dependency, if
> any].
