# Open Brain Spark (use when you don't know what to capture)

Purpose: an onboarding interview that figures out how the system fits your
specific work, then generates a personalized list of things you should be
capturing regularly. Use it once at the start, and again whenever you're
staring at a blank prompt wondering what's worth saving.

---

**Prompt:**

> Interview me briefly to figure out what's worth capturing into my personal
> memory system. Ask me, one at a time:
> 1. What tools do I use for work and thinking (chat apps, notes, code
>    editors, project trackers)?
> 2. What decisions have I made in the last two weeks that I'd hate to
>    re-explain from scratch?
> 3. Where do I keep catching myself re-explaining the same context to an AI
>    tool or a colleague?
> 4. Who are the 5-10 people I reference most often, and in what context?
>
> Once I've answered, give me a personalized list organized by category
> (decisions, people, insights, recurring topics) of specific things I should
> capture this week, phrased as ready-to-use capture sentences.
